## The pain point

Margins are under pressure — energy, labour, materials. Processes have grown over
years and have not been questioned. ERP systems formally automate; the operational
reality is 200 Excel sheets, manual handovers, double entries.

## How we differ

Fixed-price per process. Outcome measured in **EUR or hours**, not in "use cases
delivered". Comparable to lean consulting — but consistently AI-driven and operated
through Paperclip-Companies.

## Specialisation across five ERP worlds

We work explicitly with **SAP, MS Dynamics, abas, proALPHA, Sage**. For other ERPs we
say no honestly — integration depth is not infinitely scalable.

## Products

- **Efficiency Scan** (3 weeks): structured analysis of 5–8 core processes.
  Prioritised list per process.
- **Efficiency Sprint** (6–10 weeks per process): full automation of
  one process with Paperclip workers, plus RPA and API integration as needed.
- **Efficiency Subscription** (monthly per process): operation and optimisation.
