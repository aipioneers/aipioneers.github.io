## The pain point

Every retirement strips your company of 20–40 years of experience. Documentation
barely helps — the most valuable knowledge is tacit. Confluence and SharePoint did
not deliver on the promise; often they made things worse by burying content.

## What we will deliver here

- **Knowledge Audit** (€ 8 500, 4 weeks): where does which knowledge sit? Who is the
  bottleneck? Delivers a knowledge map and risk assessment.
- **Company-GPT** (€ 30 000 – 60 000, 8–14 weeks): in-house AI knowledge system built
  on existing data + structured interview-based knowledge extraction. Works-council-friendly
  mode as default.
- **Knowledge Subscription** (€ 1 500 – 3 500 / month): maintenance, expansion, new
  sources, model updates.
